How to play

  • Slide — arrow keys, WASD, or swipe. Every tile moves as far as it can.
  • Merge — two equal tiles colliding fuse into one, doubling its value.
  • Score — each merge adds the new tile’s value to your score. Best is saved locally.
  • Win — build the 2048 tile, then keep going for a higher score if you dare.
  • Lose — the board fills up and no slide can move. New game starts over.
