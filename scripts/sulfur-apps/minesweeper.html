How to play

  • Goal — reveal every safe cell on the 12×12 field. 20 mines are hidden.
  • Numbers — tell you how many mines touch that cell.
  • Safe start — mines are placed after your first click, never on it or its neighbors.
  • Flags — right-click (or long-press on touch) cycles flag → ? → none. The counter shows mines minus flags.
  • Chording — tap a revealed number whose flags match: it reveals the rest of its neighbors.
  • Flag mode — the ⚑ button makes taps place flags (handy on touch). Your best clear time is saved locally.
