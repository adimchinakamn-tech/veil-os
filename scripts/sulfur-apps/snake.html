How to play

  • Steer — arrow keys, WASD, swipes on the board, or the d-pad on touch screens.
  • Eat — amber dots are worth 1 point and grow the snake by one.
  • Bonus — the rose diamond is worth 5 points, but it fades after ~7 seconds.
  • Speed — every 5 foods the snake gets faster (L1, L2, …).
  • Death — walls and your own tail are fatal. P / Space pauses; Space restarts after a crash.
