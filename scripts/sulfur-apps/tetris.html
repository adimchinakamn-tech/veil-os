How to play

  • Fall — pieces drop from the top; fill a full row to clear it.
  • Score — 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 × level for 1–4 rows at once, +1 per soft-dropped cell, +2 per hard-dropped cell.
  • Level — every 10 cleared lines speeds up the fall.
  • Hold — C banks the current piece and swaps it for the held one (once per drop).
  • Controls — ← → move · ↓ soft drop · ↑ or X rotate · Z rotate counter-clockwise · Space hard drop · P pause.
  • Rotation nudges off walls with basic wall kicks; the ghost outline shows where the piece will land.
