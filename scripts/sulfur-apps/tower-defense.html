How to play

  • Build — click any empty cell (10 gold). Amber ghost = valid spot, red = blocked.
  • Waves — press Start wave, or wait: the next wave auto-starts ~2s after a wave is cleared.
  • Turrets — range 3.2 cells · 8 damage · ~2.5 shots/s. Hover a turret to see its range.
  • Enemies — red dots follow the lane; each wave adds +12 max HP and extra speed. Kills pay 5 gold.
  • Lives — 20. Every enemy that exits the lane costs one. Lose them all and it’s over.
