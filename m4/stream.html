Bring your real YouTube feed over

  1. open takeout.google.com and hit Deselect all
  2. pick YouTube, then subscriptions (and history if you want your watched list to come too)
  3. export — Google hands you a takeout-….tgz or .zip within minutes
  4. drop that archive right here — a bare subscriptions.csv or .json works too

Archives up to 64MB scan through the app's server. For a full multi-GB Takeout, use the importer on the website — it scans on-device with no size limit. The follow list stays on this device either way.
